Our Story

Mission Aviation Academy is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit DBA of Infinity Aero Club Tampa Bay, Inc., operating at Tampa North Aero Park (X39) in Wesley Chapel, Florida. Built on the legacy of the Tuskegee Airmen, MAA expands access to aviation, STEM, and entrepreneurship for underserved youth and veterans — with no degree, cost, or circumstance standing in the way.
