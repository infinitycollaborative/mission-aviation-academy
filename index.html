The Legacy

Before our story, theirs.

"The 332nd Fighter Group proved that Black pilots could fly, fight, and lead with the same skill and courage as anyone else. Their baton is in our hands now."

Ricardo Foster was personally mentored by Tuskegee Airmen. That mentorship shaped a leadership philosophy built on six traits: Courage, Perseverance, Excellence, Teamwork, Discipline, and Service. MAA carries that same standard into every classroom, every Saturday, every flight.

Our Story & Mission

Red Tail Roost — A Living Tribute

"The Tuskegee Airmen didn't just break a barrier — they redefined what was possible and proved that excellence has no color. Red Tail Roost exists so every generation after them gets to dream as big, fly as high, and do more."

The Red Tail Roost doesn't sit in a hangar. She flies — carrying the Tuskegee legacy into communities, schools, and airfields so every young person who sees her knows the sky was made for them too. One of 50+ aircraft and 100+ volunteer pilots and CFIs contributing to 1,380+ students on their first flights.
