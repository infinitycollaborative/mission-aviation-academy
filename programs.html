JROTC & ROTC Aviation Programs

MAA works with JROTC/ROTC units across Tampa Bay and Florida as a K-12 feeder partner and pre-collegiate CROP volunteer with Pasco-Hernando State College (PHSC).

For Unit Commanders

MAA offers curriculum licensing that brings FAA-credentialed drone, flight, and STEM programming directly to your unit's training schedule.

  • FAA TRUST drone certification in-unit
  • Part 107 prep for 16+ cadets
  • Aviation history and Tuskegee Airmen curriculum
  • AI innovation and entrepreneurship modules
  • PHSC dual-enrollment feeder pipeline
  • USF Air Force ROTC partnership track (100% placement record)
See Licensing Options

For Cadets & Families

JROTC cadets can enroll in Saturday programs and traditional courses individually, with MAA's program counting toward extracurricular aviation credentials.

  • Saturday drone and AI classes open to all cadets
  • AFJROTC Flight Academy scholarship pipeline ($22,500)
  • VR&E eligible for veterans and dependents
  • PHSC articulation credit toward AS degrees
  • Mentorship from LCDR Foster, USN (Ret.) and Rev. Reginald Davis, CDR, USN (Ret.) · Airline Captain (Ret.)
  • Flight Plan 2030 career coaching included
