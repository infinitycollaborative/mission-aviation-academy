How Scholarship Funding Works

Three steps from enrollment to your first class.

SUFS families submit directly through the EMA marketplace. MAA handles the rest.

1

Confirm your scholarship type

PEP, FES-UA, FES-EO, FTC, or NWSA. Each has different eligibility and coverage amounts. Contact us — we'll help you identify which applies.

2

Select a program

Choose from 24 live SUFS-approved offerings. Saturday classes, enrichment tiers, AI innovation, drone certification, and more. All available through the EMA marketplace.

3

Enroll via Flight Circle

We confirm your SUFS type, apply funds directly, and get your student into the next available class. No out-of-pocket cost for eligible programs.

Named Scholarships

Two scholarships. One legacy.

Beyond SUFS — MAA administers two named scholarships honoring the aviation pioneers who opened the sky for everyone.

Tuskegee Airman "1st Top Gun Trophy" Scholarship

A beacon of hope for underserved youth. Encouraging the 1% Mindset — that fighter pilots be judged by skill, not race. Honoring the legacy of the 332nd Fighter Group.

"The pilot shortage is the greatest economic opportunity in American history for underserved communities — and this scholarship is the open door."

Named in honor of Lt. Col. George Hardy, Class 44-H-SE, 332nd Fighter Group — Tuskegee Airman and mentor to Ricardo Foster.

Apply or Donate

Brave Bess "Never Stop Learning" Scholarship

For young female aviators. Modeled after Bessie Coleman's enduring motto — never stop learning — and her example of refusing to hear "no" as a final answer.

"Bessie Coleman went to France to earn her wings when America said no. We exist so the next Bessie doesn't have to leave."

Open to female-identifying students pursuing any MAA aviation, STEM, or entrepreneurship program. Ages 6+.
