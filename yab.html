Eight career families. Every one a high-income path.

None of these require a four-year degree. All of them are short of people right now. Salaries are BLS May 2024 medians unless noted.

Airline & Commercial Pilot

$226,600 median

Private Pilot through ATP. The full ladder, certificate by certificate.

Aircraft Mechanic (A&P)

$78,680 median

CNBC's #1 best-paying job that requires no degree. LSRM and OJT pathways covered.

Air Traffic Controller

$144,580 → $228K supervisor

The highest no-degree median in aviation.

Drone Operator (Part 107)

Up to $215K (defense sector)

Test at 14. Certificate at 16. Defense, survey, film, agriculture roles.

Avionics Technician

$75,100 median

Electronics, navigation systems, and the software that runs modern aircraft.

Military Aviation

Flight pay + BAH + pension

All service branches. GI Bill and VR&E funded paths to the cockpit.

Flight Instruction

$60–$95/hr + benefits

CFI, CFII, MEI. Stay in the cockpit while building hours toward the airlines.

Aerospace & Ground Careers

$55K–$140K+

Engineering, logistics, dispatch, operations — the careers that run the airfield.
