Ricardo "Tattoo" Foster is a retired Naval Officer, aviator, and the founder of Infinity Aero Club Tampa Bay, Inc. (iACT) and Mission Aviation Academy (MAA) — a 501(c)(3) nonprofit operating from Tampa North Aero Park (X39) in Wesley Chapel, FL.

His Flight Plan 2030 initiative has already impacted 300,000+ individuals across 4 countries through Hidden Treasures Network (HTN), with a goal of reaching one million young people through aviation, STEM, AI, and entrepreneurship by 2030.

Ricardo's approach is grounded in military discipline, faith-informed leadership, and the Entrepreneur Assembly Line model — moving students through discovery, certification, technical mastery, and market-ready entrepreneurship in one integrated pathway.
