How the course system works: Each intro course has 4 parts × 2 hours each — 8 total hours to complete Beginner level. Parts 1 & 2 run on Saturday Week 1. Parts 3 & 4 run on Saturday Week 2. Students who complete all 4 parts receive a graduation certificate. Intermediate (Parts 1–8) and Advanced (Parts 1–12) follow in subsequent months. Longer individual courses can be scheduled Mon/Wed/Fri online or in-person — contact us for details.
