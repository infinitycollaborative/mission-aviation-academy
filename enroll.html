Drone Certification
Saturday Classes A & C · Ages 6+ · 4-part course
$99/class
SUFS scholarship covers this class for eligible families
  • Class A (8–10 AM): FAA TRUST certification, theory of flight, drone safety & regulations
  • Class C (3–5 PM, Week 2): hands-on drone flight drills and simulator practice
  • Parts 1–4 = Beginner graduation certificate
  • Intermediate and Advanced levels follow in subsequent months
  • 8 computers provided · drones provided · drill logbook included

PPL Simulator Sessions
Available all day Saturday 8 AM–4 PM · Individual slots
$99/hour
6 slots available each Saturday · book in advance
  • FAA-approved BATD flight simulator — CFI present at every session
  • 1-hour slots: 15 min pre-flight theory, 30 min stick time, 15 min debrief
  • CFI signs your private pilot logbook every session
  • Maximum 2 students per session
  • Toward Private Pilot certificate — counts as loggable flight time

Flight Plan 2030

"To impact ONE MILLION LIVES by 2030 through aviation, STEM education, and entrepreneurship — honoring the legacy of the Tuskegee Airmen."

— LCDR Ricardo "Tattoo" Foster, USN (Ret.) · Founder & CEO, Mission Aviation Academy

Every enrollment, every SUFS scholarship family, every Saturday class advances one campaign: 1,000,000 youth impacted and $10,000,000 raised by 2030. Since 2020: $790,000+ raised · 730+ FAA credentials · 1,380+ students flown.
