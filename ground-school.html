The Pathway

From Zero to Certificate

Ground school is the knowledge foundation for every certificate in aviation. At MAA, it's integrated into the full pathway — from your first Saturday visit to your Private Pilot Certificate and beyond.

1
Saturday Open House Free
Meet the team, tour the facility at X39, and take a discovery flight. No experience needed. Ages 13+.
2
Ground School & Knowledge Test Prep You Are Here
FAA-required aeronautical knowledge: regulations, weather, navigation, aerodynamics, aircraft systems, airport operations. Covers the 60-question Private Pilot Knowledge Test and prepares students for Instrument, Commercial, and beyond.
3
Discovery Flight
Hands-on time in the cockpit of N7480X, our 1960 Cessna 172B at Tampa North Aero Park. First flight credit applied toward your training.
4
Student Pilot Certificate
FAA medical, solo flight requirements, and first solo. The moment the runway becomes yours.
5
Private Pilot Certificate & Beyond
Instrument Rating, Commercial Certificate, CFI, ATP — the full stack. MAA tracks every credential earned.
Curriculum

What Ground School Covers

The FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test draws from these subject areas. MAA ground school builds a working understanding of each — not just test answers, but real aeronautical knowledge you'll carry in the cockpit.

📋
Federal Aviation Regulations
FARs governing private pilot operations, airspace, right-of-way, and currency requirements.
🌤
Weather & Meteorology
METARs, TAFs, AIRMETs, SIGMETs, fronts, thunderstorms, icing, and pilot weather decisions.
🗺
Navigation
Sectional charts, VOR, GPS, dead reckoning, pilotage, and cross-country flight planning.
✈️
Aerodynamics
Lift, drag, thrust, weight, stalls, spins, performance, weight and balance calculations.
⚙️
Aircraft Systems
Powerplant, fuel, electrical, pitot-static, flight instruments, and emergency procedures.
🏁
Airport & Airspace
Class A–G airspace, NOTAMs, airport signs, lighting, traffic patterns, and radio procedures.
🧠
Human Factors & Physiology
Hypoxia, spatial disorientation, fatigue, aeronautical decision-making (ADM), and CRM.
🔢
Performance & E6B
Takeoff/landing distances, density altitude, fuel planning, and flight computer calculations.
💰
