The 5 SUFS Programs MAA Accepts

MAA is an approved provider under all five Step Up For Students scholarship programs as of January 2026.

PEP
NO INCOME LIMIT
Personalized Education Program. Up to $24,000 rollover. Quarterly funding cycles. Available to all Florida families.
FES-UA
IEP / DISABILITY
Family Empowerment Scholarship for Unique Abilities. Drone certification explicitly added as eligible elective in 2025–26. For students with documented disabilities.
FES-EO
INCOME-BASED
Family Empowerment Scholarship for Educational Options. Income-eligible families. MAA programs qualify as supplemental enrichment and elective providers.
FTC
INCOME-BASED
Florida Tax Credit Scholarship. Corporate-funded. Income-eligible families. MAA qualifies for enrichment and elective services.
NWSA
NEWEST PROGRAM
New Worlds Scholarship Account. Newest SUFS program. Eligible expense categories align with PEP. MAA qualifies for enrichment and elective services.

How to apply for SUFS

MAA walks every family through this process. You never navigate it alone.

1
Talk to MAA Admissions First
Book a free 30-minute admissions call. We'll confirm your eligibility, identify the right SUFS program, and walk you through the application before you start it. No paperwork required to begin.
2
Apply Through SUFS — We'll Guide You
Applications are submitted through the SUFS Education Marketplace Account (EMA) at stepupforstudents.org. New application windows open approximately February 1 each year for the following school year. MAA can provide guidance on navigating the portal.
3
Scholarship Approved — Enroll in MAA Programs
Once your SUFS scholarship is approved, funds are deposited in quarterly cycles (July, October, January, April). You select MAA programs directly through the EMA marketplace. Payment goes directly to MAA — $0 out of pocket for eligible families.
4
Start Flying, Building, and Earning Credentials
Your student attends Saturday classes at Tampa North Aero Park (X39). Discovery flights, drone labs, AI app building, and simulator sessions all run the same day. Credentials are issued directly by the FAA and MAA upon course completion.
