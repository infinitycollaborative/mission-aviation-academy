Does my family qualify?

Three questions. Takes less than 30 seconds.

Four scholarship types. One program.

MAA is approved for all four SUFS scholarship categories. Most families qualify for at least one.

FES-UA

Family Empowerment Scholarship — Unique Abilities

Amount: Up to $22,000 / year

Who qualifies: Students with an IEP, 504 plan, or documented exceptional need

Sensory-friendly, self-paced Saturday program. Our highest-conversion scholarship.

FES-EO

Family Empowerment Scholarship — Equal Opportunity

Amount: Based on household income

Who qualifies: Income-eligible Florida families (most qualify)

Covers the majority of MAA program fees. Most families pay $0.

PEP

Personal Education Pathways

Amount: Based on student needs

Who qualifies: Florida students with an IEP or exceptionality documentation

Works alongside FES-UA for students with layered support needs.

FTC

Family Tax Credit

Amount: Tax credit to contributing businesses

Who qualifies: Businesses that fund scholarships through approved SGOs

Ask your employer — they may be able to fund your child's MAA enrollment.

How enrollment works

1

Call or text

Reach us at 813-445-8889 or (833) FLY-STEM. We'll confirm your scholarship type and answer every question.

2

Enroll on SUFS EMA

We walk you through the Step Up For Students portal (EMA). Takes about 15 minutes. We do it with you.

3

Funds deposit in 2–5 days

SUFS pays MAA directly. Most families pay $0 at that point. Your student starts on the next available Saturday.

4

Student builds a plan

Day one: an individualized training blueprint. Your student's path is mapped to their goals — not a generic curriculum.
