September 11, 2001

We Remember.

Twenty-five years
September 11, 2026  ·  Wesley Chapel, Florida

Twenty-five years ago today, I was a student Naval aviator in Pensacola. I had just arrived. I had worked hard to get there, and I was finally learning how to fly.

Then the towers fell.

I remember exactly where I was standing. It was devastating, and it broke my heart. I have carried that morning with me every day since.

Today we pause to honor the lives lost, the families forever changed, the survivors who still carry it, and everyone who answered the call to serve in the years that followed.

We remember. We honor. We will never forget.

Twenty-eight years in uniform. Every one of them an honor and a privilege.

And on this solemn day, I am humbled to share something rooted in hope for the future. Our new website went live about an hour ago.

It is more than a website. It is a platform built for a mission — to change lives, open doors, inspire the next generation, and make a real difference in communities around the world. Now we can build a legacy that lasts.

Today we remember what happened. We pray for the survivors. We pray for the families and the military personnel who have carried these twenty-five years. We keep praying for them all, in Jesus' name, amen.

LCDR Ricardo "Tattoo" Foster, USN (Ret.)
Founder & CEO, Mission Aviation Academy
Semper Fi
