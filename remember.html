September 11, 2001

We Remember.

Twenty-five years

On September 11, we pause to honor and remember the lives lost, the families forever changed, the survivors, and everyone who answered the call to serve in the years that followed.

We remember. We honor. We will never forget.

Where I was

I remember exactly where I was that day, twenty-five years ago. I was a student Naval aviator in Pensacola. I'd just arrived, I had worked hard to get there, and I was finally learning how to fly.

When I saw what happened, it was incredible and it was devastating, all at once. It broke my heart.

I've carried these years with me ever since, and it's been an honor and a privilege to serve twenty-eight of them.

Building forward

On this solemn day, I'm humbled to share something rooted in hope for the future. Our new website is live, and with it we're able to build a legacy that will last.

This is more than a website. It's a platform built for a mission: to change lives, open doors, inspire the next generation, and make a real difference in communities around the world.

Today, we remember what happened on this tragic day. We pray for the survivors. We pray for the families. We just pray for them. Thank you.

LCDR Ricardo "Tattoo" Foster, USN (Ret.)
Founder & CEO, Mission Aviation Academy
Never Forget.
Keep Serving. Keep Building. Keep Impacting Lives.
Thank you to everyone who helped make this day possible.
