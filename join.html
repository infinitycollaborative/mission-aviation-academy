CONTRACT POSITION
Certified Flight Instructor (CFI)
Independent contractor · Certificate instruction through partner schools · Part 61 or Part 141 · X39

MAA needs CFIs who believe in what this mission stands for. MAA-affiliated CFIs deliver certificate flight instruction through partner schools — Central Florida Flight Training and Wings of an Angel Aviation. You'll work with SUFS-funded students and be part of the team that built a 90–95th percentile Part 107 pass rate. MAA's own Cessna 172 (N7480X) is used for discovery flights and flying club rental only.

  • CFI or CFI/CFII/MEI certification required
  • Part 61 flexible pathway experience preferred
  • Certificate instruction conducted through partner school aircraft
  • FlightCircle student management platform used
  • Independent contractor structure — rate and scope per written agreement
  • Scheduling coordinated with MAA Program Director

CONTRACT POSITION
Drone Instructor
Independent contractor · Part 107 required · X39

Lead MAA's Drone Bootcamp — a 54-hour program built on the Read → Watch → Do → Fly method. You'll teach beginner through advanced levels, support Part 107 prep, and help students earn one of the fastest credentials in aviation.

  • FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate required
  • Drone instruction or UAS operations experience
  • Beginner, intermediate, and advanced level teaching
  • Bootcamp and Saturday session availability
  • LAANC, mapping, commercial ops experience a plus

COMPENSATION STRUCTURE

Volunteer first — paid when funded

Every intern starts as a volunteer. Paid contractor roles activate when MAA secures funded contracts. The path is transparent — here's exactly how it works.

WHERE EVERYONE STARTS
Volunteer Intern
  • Nonprofit volunteer service — unpaid
  • Official Letter of Service on completion
  • LinkedIn recommendation from Ricardo
  • Portfolio artifacts filed in iACT Drive
  • Intro flight opportunity at 80+ hours
  • Part 107 training materials access
  • AFJROTC scholarship referral eligibility
  • 8-week commitment minimum
WHEN FUNDING ACTIVATES
Paid Contractor Intern
  • Activated by funded contract — not automatic
  • Independent contractor structure
  • Rate and scope defined per written agreement
  • 1099 required — contractor agreement signed first
  • All volunteer benefits still apply
  • Track 05 (Funding/Marketing) most common path
  • Subject to Ricardo's written approval
