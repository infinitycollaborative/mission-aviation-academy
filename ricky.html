The story

Ricky is grieving the loss of his great-grandfather, William — the man he called William the Great. When a neighbor's restored P-51 becomes a doorway back to 1927, Ricky meets a boy named Billy, working in a hangar to help build the Spirit of St. Louis.

By the time the plane's engine catches, Ricky understands who Billy really is, and carries home the same words his great-grandfather always told him: when a person puts their mind to it, pretty much anything can happen.

By Ricardo Foster, LCDR USN (Ret.). Coming soon through Mission Aviation Academy.

Red Tail Roost

Ricky the Explorer is part of the Red Tail Roost educational program — a traveling Tuskegee Airmen aviation tribute and youth STEM outreach initiative under Mission Aviation Academy. The book carries that mission into the hands of the youngest readers.
