Flight Simulator Setup Guide

The current MAA-recommended equipment list — from a $150 starter rig to the full Red Tail Roost club configuration. Start at Tier 1 and advance naturally as your skills grow.

Tier 1 — Starter

Begin here ~$150–$400

Runs on most Windows or Mac computers made after 2018. Perfect entry point for ages 11–17.

Software
Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 (Standard) ↗
Available on Xbox Game Pass — lowest-cost entry
Stick/Yoke
Logitech G Saitek X52 Flight Stick
Budget-friendly throttle + stick combo
Rudder
Logitech G Saitek Pro Flight Rudder Pedals
Optional at Tier 1 — add when ready to advance
Display
1080p monitor 24"+ (or existing laptop)
Minimum 1920×1080 recommended

Tier 2 — Intermediate

MAA Saturday Academy ~$500–$1,200

Used in MAA Saturday sessions at Tampa North Aero Park (X39). Mirrors what cadets use in the simulator room.

Software
X-Plane 12 (Full Version) ↗
FAA-approved flight training device data. Recommended for serious students.
Yoke
Honeycomb Aeronautical Alpha Flight Controls Yoke ↗
Used in MAA classroom. Authentic C172 feel.
Throttle
Honeycomb Bravo Throttle Quadrant ↗
Pairs with Alpha yoke. Multi-engine capable.
Rudder
MFG Crosswind V3 Rudder Pedals
Best build quality in the mid-range
PC
Intel i7 / RTX 3060 / 32GB RAM (Win 11) or MacBook Pro M2/M3
X-Plane 12 runs natively on Apple Silicon

Tier 3 — Advanced / Club

Red Tail Roost config ~$2,000–$5,000

Full Red Tail Roost simulator configuration. For students pursuing PPL or instrument rating.

Software
X-Plane 12 + Laminar Research C172 add-on
Match aircraft to the Red Tail Roost (1960 Cessna 172B, N7480X)
Avionics
RealSimGear G1000 NXi Touch Panel ↗
Garmin G1000 replica — directly transferable to glass-cockpit aircraft
Display
Triple 27" 1080p monitors OR one 4K 43" curved display
Triple setup recommended for immersive visual field
Enclosure
Obutto Ozone Gaming Cockpit (modified for flight)
Mounts yoke, throttle, and panels in correct position

Setup Tips from MAA Instructors

📐
Position matters
Yoke center should be at elbow height when seated upright. Rudder pedals flat on the floor, heel resting — same as a real C172.
🎧
Add ATC audio
Enable ATC audio in X-Plane and plug in headphones. You'll hear real phraseology — direct preparation for your first radio call.
📋
Use a real checklist
Print or display a Cessna 172 checklist beside your sim. The habit you build here transfers directly to the aircraft.
🗒️
Log your hours
Keep a simple spreadsheet or use SimToolkitPro. MAA instructors may count structured sim time toward your ground school curriculum.
