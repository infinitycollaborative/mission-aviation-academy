About TPA Career Connection

TPA Career Connection (TPA-CC) is a cohort-based aviation and STEM career development program developed by Mission Aviation Academy (MAA) in partnership with Tampa International Airport. Participants receive structured career guidance, hands-on aviation exposure, SUFS scholarship enrollment support, and direct access to airport industry professionals.

TPA-CC operates in cohorts tied to TPA Career Foundations Week and other TPA programs. Each cohort is limited to ensure individualized attention and industry access for every participant.
