The Program

What Your Child Gets

Every session is structured, hands-on, and logged. Students ages 10–17 build a real ICP Aviazione kit aircraft — from raw aluminum to a completed airframe — using the same curriculum standards as FAA-certificated mechanics.

Every Session

Learn It · Do It · Earn It

Every build session follows a three-part structure developed from EAA educational standards and adapted for youth aerospace builders.

1

Learn It

Complete an EAA online lesson at the computer station — blueprints, procedures, and theory first.

2

Do It

Apply the skill directly on the aircraft, supervised by a certified builder and credentialed instructor.

3

Earn It

Log the hours, receive your build certificate, and document your work in a portfolio for future employers.

Who Can Join

Two Tracks. One Airplane.

Students are placed into age-appropriate groups after the interview process. Both groups work on the same aircraft — each team owns a phase of the build.

Age Group A · 10–13
10–13
Young Eagles Track
  • Introduction to aircraft structure, tools, and safety
  • Foundational sheet-metal and assembly skills
  • EAA Young Eagles curriculum integration
  • Paired with an adult mentor for each session
  • Build certificate upon quarterly completion
Age Group B · 14–17
14–17
Red Tail Mechanics Track
  • Advanced airframe assembly and systems integration
  • Logged build hours toward A&P credential pathway
  • FAA-standard documentation and work-order practice
  • Direct feeder into the 12-month Workforce Track
  • Portfolio and recommendation letter upon completion
